## LinkedIn / portfolio blurb

Visceral connects an interactive 3D anatomy explorer to a reproducible WHO disease-burden pipeline covering 15.5 million observations across 185 countries. Its executed notebooks examine how age structure, sex and analytical choices change the apparent body profile of burden, including roughly 15% lower brain death ASDRs under broad age bins than WHO's fine-age method at the country median in 2023.
